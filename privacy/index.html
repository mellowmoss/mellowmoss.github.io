Privacy policy

Effective September 23, 2026. Applies to Paws Out and other games published by Mellowmoss Games ("we", "us").

The short version

You can play our games without an account. We don't ask for your name, email, location, contacts or photos. Your game progress is saved on your own device. Our games show ads and offer optional purchases; the companies that provide those services handle some data as described below.

Information stored on your device

Your progress (levels, stars, coins, boosters, lives and settings) is saved locally on your device so you can continue where you left off. It is not sent to us. Deleting the game deletes this data.

Advertising

Our games show ads through Google AdMob, and may use other ad networks through AdMob mediation. To show and measure ads and prevent fraud, these providers may collect your device's advertising identifier, IP address, device type, operating system, app usage related to ads, and approximate location derived from your IP address.

On iPhone and iPad, we only allow personalized ads if you choose "Allow" when the app asks for permission to track. If you choose "Ask App Not to Track", ads are not personalized using that identifier. On Android, you can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of ads personalization, in your device's settings. Where required by law (for example in the EEA, UK and Switzerland), we ask for your consent before personalized ads are shown.

Learn how Google uses information: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Purchases

Optional purchases (such as coins, the starter bundle or removing ads) are processed by Apple's App Store or Google Play. We never see your payment card details. We use RevenueCat to verify purchases and to restore them on a new device; it receives purchase receipts and an anonymous app user ID, not your name or payment details. RevenueCat's privacy policy: revenuecat.com/privacy.

Children

Our games are not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information through our games, contact us and we will help remove it.

Your choices and rights

You can limit ad tracking in your device settings as described above, and delete all locally stored game data by deleting the game. Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access or delete personal data held by the providers above; you can contact them directly or ask us for help.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the effective date above.
